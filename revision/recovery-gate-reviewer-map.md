# What the recovery experiment addresses

This map covers the new recovery study, alongside the earlier reconstruction. It does not assert that every reviewer concern is resolved or that the manuscript has already been rewritten. Read the prospective [protocol](recovery-gate-protocol.md), [results](recovery-gate-results/README.md) and [interpretation](recovery-gate-findings.md) together.

| Scientific concern | Executed design / implementation | Remaining boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Why should communication filtering improve routing? | Natural, sensor-observed reopenings create a need to distinguish valuable clearances from forged reports; persistent, late and no-hazard controls are retained. | These are designed synthetic mechanisms, not an empirically fitted military network. Demonstrating an opportunity does not establish learned superiority. |
| Is the gate merely rejecting messages? | Bounded acceptance and rejection authority relative to SAFE; per-receiver override budget; explicit logs and a rejection-only frozen-actor ablation. | Estimated delivery risk is heuristic; the budget bounds decision count, not harm. |
| Are labels causally meaningful? | Freeze actual neural actor; force one eligible packet accepted and rejected in the complete inbox; retain later packets and future events; verify factual branch outcomes and common exogenous attacks. | Receiver marginal effects are contextual, nonadditive and need not equal fleet effects. No general identification claim under endogenous attacks. |
| Does learning become stale? | Refresh labels halfway through learned-arm adaptation; initial/refreshed models retained, internal holdouts excluded from fitting, initial-estimator fixed-actor swap. | Refresh also adds data; this does not isolate or prove staleness as the cause of earlier failures. |
| Were baselines trained fairly? | ALL, SAFE, KEEP, AGE and LEARN receive identical 300-episode warmup state and paired 300-episode adaptation tapes, for DQN/MAPPO and eight independent paired seeds. | Auxiliary gate fitting adds interactions and compute; not equal FLOPs. 600 episodes does not establish convergence. |
| Were parameters selected on test outcomes? | AGE and LEARN inference candidates selected on disjoint validation layouts/tapes; hashes and settings locked before final testing. | Default-trained actors are not retrained for each validation setting; no global optimality claim. |
| Are cost and delivery denominators consistent? | Utility includes all five convoys, destroyed and censored outcomes; raw elapsed decomposition, complete outcome accounting and paired physical schedules audited. | Utility coefficients and one-point delivery tolerance are modeling choices, not operational preferences. Time units remain model units. |
| Are small differences statistically supported? | Crossed resampling over independent training seeds and layout/episode clusters; 140 prospectively declared contrasts; correction includes 248 earlier tests conservatively. | Eight training seeds limit precision. No declaring superiority from a mean difference or an uncorrected favorable subset. |
| Can readers access and reproduce everything? | Full source, 80 final actor checkpoints, initial/refreshed gate estimators, causal labels, training/validation/test records, eight figures, offline HTML and integrity manifest in the ZIP and GitHub branch. | Exact old submitted numerical tables remain unverified and must not be carried forward. |

The broader original-topology fixes, queue/AoI accounting, adversary modes, multi-size inference and manuscript tasks remain documented in [reviewer coverage](simulator-reviewer-coverage.md). This experiment uses structured 3/4-fork held-out layouts; it is not a new large-scale convergence study, geographic validation, bandwidth-saving experiment or completed point-by-point response letter. Final manuscript claims must follow the corrected intervals in the results, including failures and unresolved comparisons.
