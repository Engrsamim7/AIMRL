## Outcomes and interpretation

Actual delivery is primary. Route-prior score averaged over all convoys is a separate descriptive risk-proxy endpoint, not survival probability. Nominal cost, elapsed time, failure/censoring, blocked entries, traffic and actual hazard uses remain separate. In the original simulator threat does not trigger stochastic destruction: delivery failures arise from blocked routes/queues/horizon. Do not equate delivery with surviving combat losses or reinterpret the route score as a probability.

Compare FRC−ALL, FRC−NONE and selected TRAVEL−FRC over six cases equally pooled, separately by scoring/sensing specification and original/fresh-graph scope, for delivery and all-convoy route score:48contrasts. Use200,000crossed paired seed/graph/environment bootstrap draws and conservative Bonferroni436 (388earlier comparisons+48new). Original-graph inference conditions on that graph; transfer resamples eight attribute-randomized graphs. Earlier published intervals are unchanged. Five training seeds and a fixed300-episode budget do not establish convergence or universal superiority.

Expected logical checks include original IQL/NC alias equality and uniform-sensing gate redundancy where fresher local observations dominate delayed peer reports. Those are checks, not requirements to obtain favorable results. Keep all negative outcomes. Before manuscript rewriting, decide which results belong to unchanged execution, the repair bundle and scientific refinements. None can retrospectively authenticate the original fixed presentation constants.
